TheNeoHeights Story

Founded in 2011 under the strong industrial legacy of Arul Group, Neo Heights was established with a vision to redefine modern construction through engineering excellence, innovation, and customer trust.

What began as a focused civil construction company has evolved into a diversified infrastructure and engineering brand delivering large-scale projects across South India. Backed by decades of manufacturing and industrial expertise since 1978, Neo Heights combines technical strength, operational efficiency, and modern project execution capabilities.

Today, our portfolio spans industrial facilities, warehouses, commercial spaces, residential developments, infrastructure works, PEB projects, MEP engineering, and interior solutions — serving some of the world’s leading brands and industries.

Frequently asked questions

What services does Neo Heights Construction provide?

Neo Heights Builders and Promoters Pvt Ltd is an EPC contractor specializing in industrial, commercial and residential construction. We deliver end-to-end Design & Build solutions, managing every stage of the project—from planning and engineering to procurement, construction, and project handover. Our expertise spans civil works, PEB structures, MEP services, interior fit-outs, and infrastructure development, ensuring quality, efficiency, and timely execution.

You can request a quote by contacting us through our website or by phone.

Do you handle industrial, commercial, and residential construction?

Yes. We deliver industrial, commercial, and select residential / institutional projects — including civil works, PEB structures, interiors, and MEP — tailored to each client’s scope and timeline.

Use the enquiry form on our Contact page, email monalisa@neoheights.com, or call +91 9940217718. Share your project type, location, and timeline and our team will respond with next steps.

Are you licensed and insured?

Neo Heights operates with the required licenses, safety systems, and insurance coverage for the scopes we undertake. Certifications and project-specific documentation can be shared during onboarding.
